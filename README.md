# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard Project-Databrick

## Overview
This project showcases a series of business intelligence reports created using **Databricks Genie Spaces**. The analysis explores retail sales performance across products, customers, stores, and time periods to uncover key business insights and trends.

## Reports Included

### 1. Daily Sales Trends Across All Products
- Analyzes daily revenue patterns from January 2023 to January 2024.
- Highlights sales volatility and category-level performance trends.
- Compares Electronics, Clothing, and Beauty product sales over time.

### 2. Sales Performance Across Customer and Product Ranges
- Examines revenue by customer age group and gender.
- Analyzes transaction volume across product categories.
- Identifies key customer demographics and purchasing behaviors.

### 3. Sales Revenue by Product Category
- Compares total revenue generated by Electronics, Clothing, and Beauty products.
- Evaluates category contribution to overall business performance.
- Demonstrates a balanced product portfolio across categories.

### 4. Store Revenue Performance
- Analyzes revenue performance across multiple store locations.
- Identifies top-performing stores and monthly revenue trends.
- Evaluates transaction volume and average transaction value by location.
